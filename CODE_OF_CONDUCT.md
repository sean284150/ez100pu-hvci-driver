# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and safety-conscious. Harassment, discrimination,
personal attacks, publication of private card/user data, and pressure to bypass
Windows security controls are not acceptable.

Technical disagreement should be resolved with reproducible evidence. Reports
involving vulnerabilities or personal data must follow `SECURITY.md` rather
than being discussed in a public issue.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these rules
and may restrict participation when needed to protect contributors or users.
